# Arrow Escape Master — Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: To be added before release

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game **Arrow Escape Master**, developed and published by **FoxBoxStudio Dev**.

This document is a draft and must be reviewed before the public Google Play release, especially after final AdMob integration.

---

## 1. Overview

Arrow Escape Master is a casual puzzle game. The game is designed to be played without account registration.

The developer does not require users to create an account, submit personal information, or provide login credentials to play the game.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

The game developer does not directly collect:

```text
Name
Email address
Phone number
Home address
Payment card information
Account passwords
Personal messages
User-generated content
Precise GPS location
```

The game does not include:

```text
User chat
Account login
Social profile creation
Multiplayer communication
User-uploaded content
```

---

## 3. Advertising

Arrow Escape Master may display advertisements using third-party advertising services such as **Google AdMob**.

Advertising services may process certain device and usage information to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and comply with advertising policies.

This may include information such as:

```text
Advertising ID
Device information
App interaction data
Approximate usage information
Ad views
Ad clicks
Crash or diagnostic information related to ads
```

The exact data processed may depend on the advertising SDK and the user's device settings.

---

## 4. Rewarded Ads

The game may use rewarded ads.

Rewarded ads are optional. A player may choose to watch a rewarded ad to receive an in-game benefit such as:

```text
Hint
Extra chance
Double coin reward
Undo option
```

If the player does not watch the rewarded ad, the game remains playable.

---

## 5. Interstitial Ads

The game may show interstitial ads between gameplay sessions, such as after completing several levels.

Interstitial ads are not intended to appear during active gameplay.

---

## 6. In-Game Progress Data

The game may store basic progress data locally on the user's device, such as:

```text
Unlocked level
Current level
Coin balance
Sound settings
Vibration settings
Completed levels
```

This data is used only to make the game work correctly and preserve player progress.

For the first version, this progress data is expected to be stored locally on the device.

---

## 7. Children and Families

Arrow Escape Master is designed as a general casual puzzle game.

The game does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

If the game is later targeted to children or included in a family program, this Privacy Policy and advertising configuration must be reviewed and adjusted to match the applicable platform requirements.
