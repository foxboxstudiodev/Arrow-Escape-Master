Privacy Policy

Game: Arrow Escape Master

Developer: FoxBoxStudio Dev

Effective date: To be added before release

Overview

Arrow Escape Master is a casual puzzle game. The game is designed to be played without account registration.

Information We Do Not Directly Collect

The developer does not directly require users to provide a name, email address, phone number, home address, payment card information, account password, personal messages, user-generated content, or precise GPS location to play the game.

Advertising

Arrow Escape Master may display advertisements using third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. Advertising services may process certain device and usage information to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and comply with advertising policies.

This may include advertising ID, device information, app interaction data, approximate usage information, ad views, ad clicks, and diagnostic information related to ads.

Rewarded Ads

The game may use rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional. A player may choose to watch a rewarded ad to receive an in-game benefit such as a hint, extra chance, double coin reward, or undo option.

Local Game Progress

The game may store basic progress data locally on the user's device, such as unlocked level, current level, coin balance, sound settings, vibration settings, and completed levels.

Children

Arrow Escape Master is designed as a general casual puzzle game. The game does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The game may use third-party services, including Google AdMob and Google Play services where required by the Android build or advertising configuration.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information. Third-party advertising providers may process data according to their own policies for advertising, fraud prevention, analytics, and service operation.

User Choices

Users may control advertising personalization through their device settings and Google account settings where available. Users may delete the game from their device to remove locally stored game progress.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game changes, especially when new services, analytics, ads, or monetization features are added.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact FoxBoxStudio Dev.
